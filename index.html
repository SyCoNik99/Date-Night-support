Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a date idea?
Open the app, choose a category, and tap generate. You'll get a fresh date idea each time.

How do I add a date to my calendar?
When you find a date you like, you can save it to your device calendar. The app will ask for calendar permission the first time so it can add events for you.

What does a subscription include?
A subscription unlocks Pro categories and removes ads. You can choose monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?
Go to the app's settings and tap "Restore Purchases" to recover a subscription on a new device or after reinstalling.
